- [Thinking clearly about software | sean goedecke (January 2025)](https://www.seangoedecke.com/thinking-clearly/) - To think clearly, slow down and think the problem through. Thinking slowly allows you to see things more systematically and to better understand the why
- [The post-mortem problem | incident.io (March 2026)](https://incident.io/blog/the-post-mortem-problem) - A look at how post-mortem processes can fail, what good post-mortems should look like, why you should have these, as well as how AI can help or harm the process
- [Appearing Productive in The Workplace | No One's Happy (May 2026)](https://nooneshappy.com/article/appearing-productive-in-the-workplace/) - Generative AI is enabling people to produce large volumes work that they lack the expertise to review. A more disciplined use of AI would be to use it for tasks where a human has the skills to verify the correctness of its output
- [In praise of quitting | Cate Hall (August 2025)](https://usefulfictions.substack.com/p/in-praise-of-quitting) - "The far, far greater danger is in devoting our days to something that fundamentally doesn’t matter to us, because we’re too afraid to cut our losses."
