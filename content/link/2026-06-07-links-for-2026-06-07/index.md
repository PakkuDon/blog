Happy belated [World Tetris Day](https://www.internationaldays.co/event/world-tetris-day/r/rechlQcqrAiq0s43U)! Here's a bunch of links and reposts to celebrate

- [The Tetris Company Celebrates World Tetris Day with New Ways to Play, Global Fan Activations and Award-Winning Partnerships | The Tetris Company (June 2026)](https://tetris.com/news/the-tetris-company-celebrates-world-tetris-day) - A press release from the Tetris Company announcing what promos and events they had lined up to celebrate
- [Hard Drop Tetris Wiki](https://harddrop.com/wiki/Tetris_Wiki) - A Tetris wiki by fans. Covers all the Tetris games, gameplay mechanics, strategies, and other things
- [Tetresse](https://tetresse.harddrop.com/) - A tool to practice [finesse](https://harddrop.com/wiki/Finesse). If you place a piece with more than the minimum key presses required the piece will be reset and the game will suggest a more efficient sequence
- [Classic Tetris | YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ClassicTetris) - Home of the Classic Tetris World Championships
- [Repost] [TETR.IO](https://tetr.io/) - A free online stacker game with single and multiplayer modes
- [Repost] [Study shows that digital treatment with Tetris gameplay can dramatically reduce trauma memories | University of Oxford (February 2026)](https://www.ox.ac.uk/news/2026-02-19-study-shows-digital-treatment-tetris-gameplay-can-dramatically-reduce-trauma) - A study found that playing Tetris drastically reduced the prevalence of intrusive memories and other PTSD-related symptoms
